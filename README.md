# 🍫 Global Chocolate Sales Performance Dashboard

## 🎯 Business Overview & Problem Statement
This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of global chocolate sales operations across **6 markets**, **25 sales experts**, and **22 product ranges**, tracking revenue growth, regional top performers, and agent productivity across **3,282 total orders**.



## 🔑 Key Metrics & Insights
* **Total Revenue Generated:** **$19.79M** across 540K units shipped.
* **Sales Growth Trajectory:** Steady upward momentum from $6.18M (2022) to **$6.96M (2024)**.
* **Top Product:** **Smooth Sliky Salty** leading sales at **$1.12M**.
* **Top Market:** **Australia** is the highest performing region with **$3.6M** in sales revenue.
* **Top Sales Agent:** **Ches Bonnell** generating **$1.02M**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI
* **Visualizations:** Area Line Charts, Horizontal Bar Charts, KPI Cards, Interactive Slicers (Country, Product, Sales Person, Year)
